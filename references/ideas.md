# 50 "In this world" ideas

Each idea is the rule (beat 1) plus the turn (beat 4). Claude writes the two examples, the stakes and the twist.
Use them as they are, or as the bar for fresh ideas. 

## Body and life
1. In this world, your hair changes color every time you lie. One day, a boy's hair turns every color at once.
2. In this world, everyone is born with a number on their hand that counts down. One baby is born with no number.
3. In this world, your shadow does what you really want to do. One day, a girl's shadow walks away.
4. In this world, you shrink a little every time you're rude. One morning, the meanest kid in school wakes up the size of a coin.
5. In this world, you age one year every time you sneeze. Then flu season starts.
6. In this world, your reflection is always one second late. One day, it's one second early.
7. In this world, everyone can hear your thoughts, except your best friend. Then she starts to hear them too.
8. In this world, people glow when they're happy. One town hasn't glowed in ten years.
9. In this world, your height shows how much money you have. One day, a beggar grows taller than the king.
10. In this world, the first word you say as a baby becomes your power. One baby's first word is "no."

## Town and rules
11. In this town, the sun rises once every four years. This time, something else rises.
12. In this town, it rains whatever you're afraid of. One day, it rains clocks.
13. In this town, every door opens to a random house. One door opens to the inside of a giant's pocket.
14. In this town, the lights go out at nine, and nobody knows why. One night, someone turns them on.
15. In this town, everyone gets one wish on their birthday. One boy saves his wish for ten years.
16. In this town, the streets move at night. One morning, the school is gone.
17. In this town, it's against the law to smile. Then a clown moves in.
18. In this town, the mayor is chosen by a dragon. This year, the dragon chooses a baby.
19. In this town, every house has a door you must never open. One kid opens it.
20. In this town, the clock tower decides when you go to sleep. One night, it stops.

## School and family
21. In this world, kids are graded on how brave they are. One shy girl gets the highest score ever.
22. In this school, the teacher is a robot that never forgets. One day, it forgets everyone's name.
23. In this world, your parents are picked by a lottery every year. This year, a boy gets the king and queen.
24. In this world, you get a pet the day you turn ten. One boy gets a pet that's still hatching.
25. In this school, the exam decides your job forever. One student gets a job that doesn't exist.
26. In this world, kids rule and adults go to school. One day, the adults want their power back.
27. In this world, grandmas are the strongest fighters. Then an army attacks a nursing home.
28. In this school, the lunch lady can see the future in the soup. One day, she drops the ladle.
29. In this world, siblings share one heart. One day, the little brother runs away.
30. In this world, homework does itself, but it remembers who skipped it.

## Monsters and magic
31. In this world, monsters are afraid of kids. One monster isn't.
32. In this world, every child gets a guardian dragon. One child's dragon is the size of a mouse.
33. In this forest, the trees remember everyone who walks past. One tree starts talking.
34. In this world, ghosts have jobs. One ghost gets fired.
35. In this world, the moon is a giant eye that closes once a year. Tonight, it doesn't close.
36. In this castle, the king changes every time the clock strikes twelve. At midnight, the clock strikes thirteen.
37. In this world, slime is money. Then it starts to rain slime.
38. In this world, every mirror is a door. One mirror breaks.
39. In this sea, the fish vote for the king of the beach. This year, they vote for a shark.
40. In this world, magic only works when you whisper. One girl can only shout.

## Money and power
41. In this world, you pay with minutes of your life. One man is the richest person alive and has one minute left.
42. In this world, the richest person is the one with the most friends. One boy has zero, until today.
43. In this world, everyone gets a mystery box on their birthday. One box is empty, and it's the most dangerous one.
44. In this world, crowns choose their kings. One crown chooses a dog.
45. In this world, gold turns to dust if you're greedy. One king touches his treasure.
46. In this world, every lie costs one coin. One politician goes broke in a day.
47. In this city, the tallest building belongs to whoever climbs it first. Today, a toddler starts climbing.
48. In this world, you can sell your sleep. One boy hasn't slept in a year.
49. In this world, a countdown appears over anyone about to become famous. One morning, it appears over the whole town.
50. In this world, everyone has a price tag on their head. One girl's tag says "priceless."
